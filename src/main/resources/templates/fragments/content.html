About Us

Welcome to The Midnight Notebook

Why was this Created?

Originally this website was for poems only. Poems are raw expressions of memory and experience. Truth can be seaved from these. My hope was to distance me and other seekers for creating work which is authentic and rightly ordered. If there is any sense of truth in you and can't find it in pop culture or agenda based art, this place is for you. The poems give the material and the essays and blogs refine it. Later on I plan to make a mechanism for synthesizing various categories and genres to create a system for understanding the world.

Contact me by Email @edavidpinedam@gmail.com
