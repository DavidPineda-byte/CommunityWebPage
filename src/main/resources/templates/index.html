Where Words Learn to Burn

There are moments that ask for silence.
For a flame.
For language that does not rush to explain itself.

This is a small studio of poems and candles made for those moments.

A Quiet Practice

This began as a habit — writing at night, lighting a candle, letting the room hold what the day could not. Over time, the poems stayed. The wax melted. The ritual remained.

Each candle is hand-poured slowly, in small batches. Each poem is written with the expectation that it will be read in stillness. They are not meant to impress. They are meant to accompany.

Nothing here is rushed. Nothing is made to be loud.
These are objects for people who listen.

The Shoreline Series

Some things return again and again — memory, distance, the sense of standing at the edge of something unfinished.

The Shoreline Series pairs soft, open scents with poems about leaving, waiting, and the strange comfort of horizons. Made for evenings when the mind drifts and the past feels close enough to touch.

Mist

Mist was created for moments of uncertainty — when direction is unclear, but movement continues. A light, clean burn paired with a short poem about beginnings, hesitation, and the beauty of not knowing what comes next.

Mountains

This piece carries weight. A deeper scent, slower to unfold, paired with poetry about endurance, solitude, and the quiet strength of staying.

For long nights.
For early mornings.
For those who keep going without spectacle.
